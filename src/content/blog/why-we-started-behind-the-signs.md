I've been in the sign business for more than thirty years. I've run a shop, built software for shops, sat on association boards, and walked through more fabrication floors than I can count. And the thing that still surprises me is how much of what we know never gets written down.

Every shop has a person who knows the right way to seam a cabinet face, or what to say when a customer wants a lit sign for a vinyl budget, or how to price a job so the shop actually makes money on it. That knowledge lives in one head. When that person retires, or moves on, it leaves with them.

Behind the Signs is our attempt to fix that.

## What We Do

**The podcast.** Long conversations with people who run sign shops, sell to sign shops, design for them, and install for them. Not interviews about how great everyone is. Real talk about what worked, what didn't, and what they'd do differently.

**Shop Tours.** We visit shops and walk the floor with the owner. You see the equipment, the workflow, the layout, and the decisions behind them. There's a lot you can learn from ten minutes inside someone else's building.

**The community.** Our Facebook group is where the day-to-day happens. Someone posts a problem, five people who've solved it answer. If you're not in it yet, join. It's free and it's the most useful part of what we do.

**The newsletter.** Once a month, one article like this one. Something with enough depth that it's worth your time, on a topic that affects how you run your business. No filler and no daily noise.

## Who this is for

If you own a sign shop, this is for you. If you sell for one, design for one, run production for one, or hang the signs when they're done, this is for you too. The industry is bigger than the shop floor, and everyone in it has something to teach the rest of us.

## What We're Asking

Subscribe to the newsletter. That's the whole ask. It's how we know you're out there, and it's what lets us bring sponsors on board who actually serve this industry, which keeps everything else free.
