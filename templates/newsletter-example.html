Quick Tips This Week

💡

Vinyl application in cold weather? Keep your material at room temp until you're ready to apply. A cheap foam cooler works great as an insulated carrier.

📐

Quick measurement hack: Use your phone's LiDAR (iPhone 12 Pro+) for rough site surveys. Not perfect, but great for initial quotes before the official survey.

💰
